# Functional review brief and rubric

## Brief

Create task-focused Thai investment workspaces: detailed orders, portfolio content, and saved funds. Prioritize task hierarchy, readable density, selected-record context, and responsive pane behavior.

## Rubric

- Hierarchy makes the primary workspace task clear.
- Pane relationships and information density remain readable at all supplied widths.
- Thai text, values, focus states, and selected details are not clipped or obscured.
- The white-surface, hairline-boundary functional system is consistent.
